How JODO Works

A transparent four-step process connecting village buyers to authorized motorcycle dealerships.

1

Choose your bike.

Select the motorcycle or scooter you intend to purchase within the next 30 to 60 days.

2

Join nearby buyers.

Connect with buyers from your taluk and nearby villages who want the same or related vehicle.

3

JODO negotiates with dealerships.

We present collective buyer demand to authorised dealerships to negotiate group benefits, accessory bundles, and pricing concessions.

4

Compare the verified offer and purchase directly from the dealership.

Review the transparent deal sheet. Pay the dealership directly, complete official RTO paperwork, and take delivery.

How Group Buying Saves You Time and Money

Buying a two-wheeler alone from a village is tiring and expensive. Here is how buying together changes the equation.

Save Days of Travel Between Showrooms

In rural taluks, visiting 3 or 4 dealerships across towns costs multiple days of lost work and travel. JODO collects verified, written offers for your group so you only visit once to finalize.

Real Leverage Instead of Fixed Retail Markups

An individual buyer walking into a showroom has almost zero bargaining power against mandatory accessory bundles and handling fees. A group of 15–25 buyers gives dealerships strong incentive to offer fleet-level value.

Safe & Direct: 100% Dealership-Billed

JODO never touches your vehicle purchase money. Every rupee is paid directly by you at the authorised showroom counter, with official GST invoice, manufacturer warranty, and RTO registration in your name.

Local Village Community Trust

Coordinate with neighbors and peers from your own taluk. You take delivery with people you know, turning what is often an intimidating showroom visit into a shared community milestone.
